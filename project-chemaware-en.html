ChemAware

A smart chemical-safety system combining sensor hardware with a connected app to monitor gases and chemicals, send alerts, analyze exposure data, and provide simplified safety information and guidance.

View Project PDF ↗

Problem

Workers in factories, laboratories, and industrial environments may repeatedly handle chemicals without a simple way to understand exposure risks, increasing the likelihood of long-term occupational and health risks.

Target Users

  • Chemistry students and researchers.
  • Laboratory workers.
  • HSE and occupational-safety professionals.
  • Organizations that work with chemical materials.

Solution

ChemAware connects a sensor-equipped device with an app that monitors chemical gases and substances, generates alerts when unsafe levels are detected, provides material information, and simplifies SDS content.

Role of AI

  • Identify chemical materials by name or image.
  • Analyze data received from the device.
  • Simplify SDS safety information.
  • Provide safety guidance based on risk level.
