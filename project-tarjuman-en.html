TARJUMAN

A digital platform designed to connect organizations participating in international events with qualified Saudi language experts. The platform supports expert search, account approval, requests, evaluation, complaints, and deposit-based booking workflows.

TARJUMAN Website ↗

Project Idea

Tarjuman is a digital platform that connects organizations participating in international events with qualified Saudi language professionals It helps organizations find the right experts based on language field of expertise location and ratings while managing the collaboration process from search and selection to evaluation all in one clear streamlined experience

By making Saudi language expertise more accessible Tarjuman empowers local talent and supports effective communication across cultures at international events

Tarjuman Connecting Saudi language expertise with the world

Key Features

  • Account approval and profile management.
  • Expert search by language, specialty, city, and rating.
  • Evaluation and complaint workflows.
  • Request tracking between organizations and experts.

System Analysis

The project included requirements analysis, workflow definition, user roles, database modeling, and interface design to create a complete digital service flow.
